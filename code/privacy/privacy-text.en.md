## Automatic replies on Instagram

If you comment with a keyword under a post or reel of [@youraccount], [practice name] automatically sends you a private message with the link you asked for, and posts a short reply under your comment. We do this with our own program on our own web hosting, through the official connection of Meta (the company behind Instagram).

**What we keep:** your Instagram username and the number Instagram gives your account, the text of your comment, which post you commented on, the time, whether the message was sent, and whether you tapped the link in the message. We do not store an IP address and do not set a cookie with that link.

**Why:** to send you the message you asked for, to avoid sending the same thing twice, and to see how many people use the link. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering a request you made yourself, and [change this if you use another legal basis].

**How long:** after 90 days your username, your number and the text of your comment are erased. What remains are counts without names (so many comments, so many clicks).

**Where it is stored:** at our web host [host name], in [country or EU]. The data goes nowhere else, except that the message itself is sent through Instagram (Meta). Meta processes your data under Meta's own privacy policy.

**Erasure:** if you want us to erase your data sooner, send a message to [email address] with your Instagram username. We erase it within [number] days.
